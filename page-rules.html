Player's Handbook

Welcome to SledgehammerRP! Below is every rule the server currently employs to ensure a fair, compelling, and safe experience for all. Rules are subject to change and interpretation by staff.

0.0 - Definitions & Common Acronyms

Definition

0.1 | "Combat Logging"

The act of disconnecting intentionally during combat to escape consequence.

Definition

0.2 | 'Powergaming'

A Character declaring success in an action against another Character without having given appropriate opportunity or freedom to respond or act independently.

Definition

0.3 | 'Metagaming'

The act of a Player abusing information from Discord, Tickets, or other sources to gain an advantage.

Definition

0.4 | "Loot Cycling"

The act of a Player abusing loot respawn mechanics within the game to 'reset' the loot table for a loot container.

Definition

0.5 | 'Raiding'

Hostile actions involving the altering of a structure, vehicle, an item or any other property a Character may own.

Definition

0. | 'Mechanical'

Any action or gameplay-related event that occurs within the game server.

Definition

0. | 'Player'

A member of the SledgehammerRP Community that is whitelisted and verified.

Definition

0. | 'Character'

An original, fictional, and creative manifestation of the Player whom interacts with the server in-game.

Definition

0. | 'Staff'

A high-ranking member of the SledgehammerRP Community that holds power and the designated roles associated with such.

Acronym

0. | 'NPC'

Non-Player Character: A Character created by Staff that serves narrative, balance, or other purposes. NPCs are held to the same provisions and protections as players.

Acronym

0. | 'IC'

In-Character: The actions, information, and conduct taken and known by a particular character or group of characters

Acronym

0. | 'OOC'

Out-of-Character: The actions, information, and conduct taken and known by the Player

Acronym

0. | 'RP'

Roleplay: Assuming the role of a fictional Character and manifesting actions in-game through them

Acronym

0. | 'NVFL'

No Value For Life: Actions by a Character which do not display reasonable concern for their own life.

Acronym

0. | 'PK'

Permanent Kill: The permanent death of a Character in which they may no longer be played by a Player.

Acronym

0. | 'NLR'

New Life Rule: The state in which a Character may return to the game world after having mechanically died.

Acronym

0. | 'PVP Death'

Player vs. Player Death: The state in which a Character is mechanically killed by another Character, Player-controlled or NPC-controlled.

Acronym

0. | 'RevRP'

Revival Roleplay: Roleplay that facilitates the resuscitation or 'nullification' of NLR and a Character's PVP Death.

Acronym

0. | 'MedRP'

Medical Roleplay: Roleplay that facilitates the treatment of a Character's wounds.

Acronym

0. | 'RDM' and 'KOS'

Random Deathmatch: The act of attacking another Character without prior initiation or conflict.

Acronym

0. | 'PVP'

Player vs. Player: The state of a Character engaging in valid combat with another Character. This includes NPCs.

Acronym

0. | 'PvE'

Player vs. Environment: The state of a Character engaging in combat with Zombies/'Zeds'/'Infected', hunger, thirst, or any other environmental factor that poses an acute danger to the Character. This does not include NPCs.

1.0 - Discord & Community Guidelines

Community

1.1 | General Conduct

Harassment, toxicity, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, racism, ableism, and all other forms of bigotry are not tolerated within the SledgehammerRP Community. Debates upon political, social, or otherwise controversial issues from the real-world are prohibited. Those under the legal age of 18 years are prohibited from participating in this community until they are of age.

Community

1.2 | OOC/IC Civility

All Discord channels are considered OOC unless explicitly marked otherwise. Players are not to bring IC or in-game conflicts into Discord except within in a ticket with Staff. Collaboration and cooperation with others Players through in-game channels to resolve situations is heavily encouraged.

Community

1.3 | Game Bleed & OOC Mental Wellness

All Players are responsible for their own mental wellness. If a Player finds themselves distressed by in-game events, it is advised for the Player to take a break away from the game and/or community until the Player is in good mental health.

Staff

1.4 | Staff Authority

ALL Staff decisions are final. If a Player holds grievance with a Staff member's decision, appeal privately via the ticket system.

Discord

1.5 | English Only

SledgehammerRP is an English-only server. In order to enforce policies and moderation, all Players and members of the Discord are expected to speak English.

Discord

1.6 | Prior Conduct In Other Spaces

If a Player has an evidence-based history of problematic conduct in other spaces or servers, staff reserve the right to revoke the Player's whitelist and perform a removal of the Player at any time.

Discord

1.7 | Follow Discord's Terms of Service

Do not share content that violates Discord's terms of service.

This includes, but is not limited to: piracy, distribution of NSFW content, doxxing, or other illegal activities.

2.0 - Roleplay Standards

Fair Play

2.1 | Powergaming

The Player's actions towards others must leave opportunity for the other character to react in all cases. Players may not assume success in every action upon another. The k3ycode is perestroika. Disguises fall under this provision (see rule 2.2).

Example: "/do Marcus takes Jade's arm and forces her to the ground with a swift, singular motion." (Disallowed)

Fair Play

2.2 | Disguises

Players may opt to rename their Character temporarily for the purpose of disguise. Characters may be renamed to a description of the Character or a consistent pseudonym. Descriptions must include at least one personally-identifying detail. Speech conducted under the disguised Character must have identifying characteristics described. Players may not impersonate another Player.

Conduct

2.3 | FailRP

The Player's Character must act in accordance with the Character's personality, knowledge, and ability. Staff hold final discretion on what constitutes a breach of the FailRP provision.

Examples of FailRP include:

  • Unrealistic behavior that breaks immersion
  • Failure to appropriately react to danger or fear
  • Disregarding Character injuries

Conduct

2.4 | No Value For Life (NVFL)

The Player's Character must value their life appropriately at all times. If a Character's behavior is deemed to have been in violation of this provision, FailRP provisions apply.

Content

2.5 | Erotic Roleplay

Erotic Roleplay of any type is strictly prohibited. Players engaging in romance roleplay may opt to use a 'fade-to-black' and use implication, but may never describe overt sexual actions.

Content

2.6 | Prohibited Content

Sexual harassment, sexual assault, rape, and accusations of the former items are strictly prohibited forms of roleplay.

3.0 - Game Meta

Exploits

3.1 | Abuse of Game Mechanics (AOGM)

Using glitches, bugs, or unintended mechanics of the game to one's benefit is strictly prohibited.

This includes, but is not limited to: clipping through walls, item duplication, or shooting from a Z-level to the north or west.

Exploits

3.2 | Metagaming

Using OOC knowledge to influence a character's actions is strictly prohibited.

This includes, but is not limited to: Information from Discord calls, DMs, or lore tickets, except in cases where explicitly allowed by Staff.

Exploits

3.3 | Cheating

Use of third-party hacks, scripts, or macros are strictly forbidden.

Chat

3.4 | Overuse of /ooc In-Game

Use of the /ooc command is restricted to immediate needs for communicating with others regarding urgent OOC matters. Overuse of the command is highly discouraged. Under no circumstance is a Player allowed to use VOIP for OOC-use.

4.0 - Hostilities

PVP

4.1 | Initiation

When the Player wishes to engage in hostilities of any kind with another Player, they must hold reasonable RP justification for the act and type a valid initiation message in /shout prior to hostile action.

The initiation must include the following:

  • An addressal of the person being initiated upon
  • A reasonable demand
  • Consequences if the demand is not fulfilled

Example: "/shout You there! Put your hands up, or I will shoot!"

PVP

4.2 | Combat Logging

The act of disconnecting intently during combat, capture, or pursuit constitutes a rulebreak. Technical issues should be communicated with the immediately involved Players and Staff as soon as possible.

PVP

4.x | KOS Zones

Certain thematic settings may contain 'KOS' zones. Initiation rules are waived in these areas.

Fair Play

4.x | Fair Engagement

A Player may not exploit Z-level mechanics (see rule 3.3, AOGM), abuse designated safe zones, or other server mechanics to escape/gain an advantage in combat.

Fair Play

4.x | Downed Players

In the instance that a Player falls to PVP or another danger, they are subject to certain provisions.

  • OOC consent must be gathered prior to a 'double-tap' or execution of the player.
  • In the instance that a Player is downed after having had two prior PVP deaths, the consent clause does not apply for execution.
  • The Player who is downed by a Character must lose a PVP life.
  • Field torture or torment of a downed Player is not permissible.

Fair Play

4.x | Hostage and Interrogation of Players

If a Player is taken hostage by another Character, the Player and Character taking them hostage are subject to certain provisions.

  • OOC consent must be collected for imprisonments longer than 2 IRL hours.
  • OOC consent must be collected for torture permission prior to interrogation or torture.
  • The Player waives the ability to retract their consent as soon as it is given.
  • Execution of the captured is permissible only if consent is given in OOC and/or the captured has no remaining PVP lives.
