Player's Handbook

Welcome to SledgehammerRP! Below is every rule the server currently employs to ensure a fair, compelling, and safe experience for all. Rules are subject to change and interpretation by staff.

0.0 - Definitions & Common Acronyms
Definition

0.1 | "Combat Logging"

The act of disconnecting intentionally during combat to escape consequence.

Definition

0.2 | 'Powergaming'

A Character declaring success in an action against another Character without having given appropriate opportunity or freedom to respond or act independently.

Definition

0.3 | 'Metagaming'

The act of a Player abusing information from Discord, Tickets, or other sources to gain an advantage.

Definition

0.4 | "Loot Cycling"

The act of a Player abusing loot respawn mechanics within the game to 'reset' the loot table for a loot container.

Definition

0.5 | 'Raiding'

Hostile actions involving the altering of a structure, vehicle, an item or any other property a Character may own.

Definition

0.6 | 'Mechanical'

Any action or gameplay-related event that occurs within the game server.

Definition

0.7 | 'Player'

A member of the SledgehammerRP Community that is whitelisted and verified.

Definition

0.8 | 'Character'

An original, fictional, and creative manifestation of the Player whom interacts with the server in-game.

Definition

0.9 | 'Staff'

A high-ranking member of the SledgehammerRP Community that holds power and the designated roles associated with such.

Acronym

0.10 | 'NPC'

Non-Player Character: A Character created by Staff that serves narrative, balance, or other purposes. NPCs are held to the same provisions and protections as players.

Acronym

0.11 | 'IC'

In-Character: The actions, information, and conduct taken and known by a particular character or group of characters

Acronym

0.12 | 'OOC'

Out-of-Character: The actions, information, and conduct taken and known by the Player

Acronym

0.13 | 'RP'

Roleplay: Assuming the role of a fictional Character and manifesting actions in-game through them

Acronym

0.14 | 'NVFL'

No Value For Life: Actions by a Character which do not display reasonable concern for their own life.

Acronym

0.15 | 'PK'

Permanent Kill: The permanent death of a Character in which they may no longer be played by a Player.

Acronym

0.16 | 'NLR'

New Life Rule: The state in which a Character may return to the game world after having mechanically died.

Acronym

0.17 | 'PVP Death'

Player vs. Player Death: The state in which a Character is mechanically killed by another Character, Player-controlled or NPC-controlled.

Acronym

0.18 | 'RevRP'

Revival Roleplay: Roleplay that facilitates the resuscitation or 'nullification' of NLR and a Character's PVP Death.

Acronym

0.19 | 'MedRP'

Medical Roleplay: Roleplay that facilitates the treatment of a Character's wounds.

Acronym

0.20 | 'RDM' and 'KOS'

Random Deathmatch: The act of attacking another Character without prior initiation or conflict.

Acronym

0.21 | 'PVP'

Player vs. Player: The state of a Character engaging in valid combat with another Character. This includes NPCs.

Acronym

0.22 | 'PvE'

Player vs. Environment: The state of a Character engaging in combat with Zombies/'Zeds'/'Infected', hunger, thirst, or any other environmental factor that poses an acute danger to the Character. This does not include NPCs.

1.0 - Discord & Community Guidelines
Community

1.1 | General Conduct

Harassment, toxicity, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, racism, ableism, and all other forms of bigotry are not tolerated within the SledgehammerRP Community. Debates upon political, social, or otherwise controversial issues from the real-world are prohibited. Those under the legal age of 18 years are prohibited from participating in this community until they are of age.

Community

1.2 | OOC/IC Civility

All Discord channels are considered OOC unless explicitly marked otherwise. Players are not to bring IC or in-game conflicts into Discord except within in a ticket with Staff. Collaboration and cooperation with others Players through in-game channels to resolve situations is heavily encouraged.

Community

1.3 | Game Bleed & OOC Mental Wellness

All Players are responsible for their own mental wellness. If a Player finds themselves distressed by in-game events, it is advised for the Player to take a break away from the game and/or community until the Player is in good mental health.

Staff

1.4 | Staff Authority

ALL Staff decisions are final. If a Player holds grievance with a Staff member's decision, appeal privately via the ticket system.

Discord

1.5 | English Only

SledgehammerRP is an English-only server. In order to enforce policies and moderation, all Players and members of the Discord are expected to speak English.

Discord

1.6 | Prior Conduct In Other Spaces

If a Player has an evidence-based history of problematic conduct in other spaces or servers, staff reserve the right to revoke the Player's whitelist and perform a removal of the Player at any time.

Discord

1.7 | Follow Discord's Terms of Service

Do not share content that violates Discord's terms of service.

This includes, but is not limited to: piracy, distribution of NSFW content, doxxing, or other illegal activities.

2.0 - Roleplay Standards
Fair Play

2.1 | Powergaming

The Player's actions towards others must leave opportunity for the other character to react in all cases. Players may not assume success in every action upon another. The k3ycode is perestroika. Disguises fall under this provision (see rule 2.2).

Example: "/do Marcus takes Jade's arm and forces her to the ground with a swift, singular motion." (Disallowed)

Fair Play

2.2 | Disguises

Players may opt to rename their Character temporarily for the purpose of disguise. Characters may be renamed to a description of the Character or a consistent pseudonym. Descriptions must include at least one personally-identifying detail. Speech conducted under the disguised Character must have identifying characteristics described. Players may use voice-altering software, provided that the effects applied on microphone transmissions are not unnatural or unfitting.

Players may not impersonate another Player or a NPC.

Conduct

2.3 | FailRP

The Player's Character must act in accordance with the Character's personality, knowledge, and ability. Staff hold final discretion on what constitutes a breach of the FailRP provision.

Examples of FailRP include:

  • Unrealistic behavior that breaks immersion
  • Failure to appropriately react to danger or fear
  • Disregarding Character injuries

Conduct

2.4 | No Value For Life (NVFL)

The Player's Character must value their life appropriately at all times. If a Character's behavior is deemed to have been in violation of this provision, FailRP provisions apply.

Examples of NVFL violations include:

  • Fighting hordes of a number greater than 25 enemies by one's self
  • Being intentionally belligerent whilst being held at gun-point
  • Intentional harm or sacrifice without narrative foundation

Conduct

2.5 | Voice Etiquette

All Players are permitted to use Voice-Over-IP (VOIP) to roleplay as their Character. VOIP-Players shall yield to Text-Players and allow reasonable time between messages such that Text-Players are not ignored.

VOIP may never occur on the 101MHz or 102MHz public frequencies. VOIP is permissible over private radio channels.

Content

2.6 | Romance & Erotic Roleplay

Erotic Roleplay of any type is strictly prohibited. Players engaging in romance roleplay may opt to use a 'fade-to-black' and use implication, but may never describe overt sexual actions.



A Player Character may never engage in RomanceRP with another Player's Character without explicit OOC consent.
Content

2.7 | Prohibited Content

Players are strictly prohibited from engaging in disallowed content or roleplay. Corrective action is subject to Staff discretion.

Disallowed content includes:

  • Sexual Harassment
  • Sexual Assault
  • Rape
  • Accusation towards another of any of the above items

3.0 - Game Meta
Exploits

3.1 | Abuse of Game Mechanics (AOGM)

Using glitches, bugs, or unintended mechanics of the game to one's benefit is strictly prohibited.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Clipping through walls
  • Item duplication
  • Shooting at other Players or NPCs from a higher Z-level to the north or west
  • Walking against corners, walls, or glass to bulk-level skills.

Exploits

3.2 | Metagaming

Using OOC knowledge to influence a character's actions is strictly prohibited.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Information from Discord calls
  • Information from Discord DMs
  • Information from Lore Tickets, unless explicitly allowed by Staff

Exploits

3.3 | Cheating

Use of third-party hacks, scripts, or macros are strictly forbidden.

Chat

3.4 | Overuse of /ooc In-Game

Use of the /ooc command is restricted to immediate needs for communicating with others regarding urgent OOC matters. Overuse of the command is highly discouraged. Under no circumstance is a Player allowed to use VOIP for OOC matters.

Fair Play

3.5 | Inactive Players

Players are expected to log into the game server at least once within a two-week period to be considered active. If a Player has not logged into the game server within the specified time period, their claimed vehicles, structures, and items are considered forfeit. A Player must inform Staff via ticket if they wish to take a hiatus of two weeks or more and retain ownership of their claims.

4.0 - Hostilities
PVP

4.1 | Initiation

When the Player wishes to engage in hostilities of any kind with another Character or NPC, they must hold reasonable RP justification for the act and type a valid initiation message in /shout prior to hostile action. Defending Characters are not required to initiate. It is recommended to record the encounter in its entirety.

The initiation must include the following:

  • An addressal of the person being initiated upon
  • A reasonable demand
  • Consequences if the demand is not fulfilled

Example: "/shout You there! Put your hands up, or I will shoot!"

PVP

4.2 | Combat Logging

The act of disconnecting intently during combat, capture, or pursuit constitutes a rulebreak. Technical issues should be communicated with the immediately involved Players and Staff as soon as possible.

PvP

4.3 | Factions & Mass Hostilities

In the instance that a Faction or Group of players wish to enter combat with another party, only one member of the Faction or Group within the immediate area is required to initiate properly. The defending parties in this instance would be considered the immediate allies within the vicinity of the primary party being initiated upon, including those who may not necessarily be within the same Group or Faction as the party being initiated upon.

PVP

4.4 | KOS Zones

Certain thematic settings may contain 'KOS' zones. Initiation rules are waived in these areas.

Fair Play

4.5 | Fair Engagement

A Player may not exploit Z-level mechanics (see rule 3.3, AOGM), abuse designated safe zones, or other server mechanics to escape/gain an advantage in combat.

Fair Play

4.6 | Downed Players

In the instance that a Player falls to PVP or another danger, they are subject to certain provisions.

  • OOC consent must be gathered prior to a 'double-tap' or execution of the player.
  • In the instance that a Player is downed after having had two prior PVP deaths, the consent clause does not apply for execution.
  • The Player who is downed by a Character must lose a PVP life.
  • Field torture or torment of a downed Player is not permissible without OOC consent.

Hostage

4.7 | Hostage and Interrogation of Players

If a Player is taken hostage by another Character, the Player and Character taking them hostage are subject to certain provisions.

  • OOC consent must be collected for imprisonments longer than 2 IRL hours.
  • OOC consent must be collected for torture permission prior to interrogation or torture.
  • The Player waives the ability to retract their consent as soon as it is given.
  • Execution of the captured is permissible only if consent is given in OOC and/or the captured has no remaining PVP lives.

Raiding

4.8 | Theft

Stealing is a permissable action that a Character may undertake. However, the Player that wishes to steal from another Character or NPC must emote the action or properly initiate upon the victim.

Raiding

4.9 | Base Raiding

In the instance that a Player or faction of Players wish to conduct an assault upon a compound or Character-owned building, the initiation must be made in reasonable listening distance to the defenders. Under no circumstance is an offline raid permissable. Raids should be recorded in their entirety by all participants where possible.

Raiding

4.10 | Raiding RP Standards

Appropriate RP during raids is considered necessary. There should be precedent for the raid, exchanges made between combatants, and emotes where possible.

5.0 - Looting, Items, & Economy
Looting

5.1 | Furniture & Tiles

Players are prohibited from taking tiles in marked lore locations, player-built structures without permission, and within spawn. Players must not destroy tiles or pick up containers with inventories without express approval from Staff in a designated ticket.

Looting

5.2 | Loot Cycling

Loot cycling or any other action that intentionally interferes with the natural respawn timer for loot within loot containers is prohibited.

Economy

5.3 | Loot Hoarding

Loot hoarding without IC justification or intent for near-term usage is prohibited. Staff hold final discretion on what constitutes a breach of this provision, and the offending Player or Players are subject to redistribution or an administrative reduction of items.

Conduct

5.4 | Item Littering

Players are not permitted to leave unreasonable amounts of items on the floor without intent for near-term use. Corrective action will be proportional to impact on server performance and health.

6.0 - Death, Medical, and Revival Roleplay
Deaths

6.1 | PvE-Related Deaths

In the event that a Player's Character is mechanically killed by PvE, the Player is granted the option to respawn, given that their behavior leading up to the mechanical death is considered reasonable by Staff and not a violation of the NVFL provisions.

Deaths

6.2 | PvP-Related Deaths

Each Character is granted a maximum of 3 lives toward PvP deaths. In the instance that a Character is mechanically killed by another Character three times total, the Character must take a Character Death. See 6.5 for provisions regarding Character Death.

Deaths

6.3 | New Life Rule (NLR)

The Player may not bring their Character to the site of their death until a minimum of 30 IRL minutes have passed since the initial death. In the instance that the Player Character's body is moved to another location away from the initial death, the Player may return to the body's location.

Deaths

6.4 | Zombie Bites

The Zombie Infection may vary depending on the season's lore and setting. A Player is given the option to consider any mechanical death to PvE causes as a 'bite' from a zombie. If the Player decides to proceed with a 'bite', they may not retract the decision.

Deaths

6.5 | Character Death

In the event that a Character suffers three (3) PVP-related deaths, the Player is prohibited from playing the Character from the incident of the third death. Players may continue to write auxillary material relating to the Character. All Character Deaths are final and may not be reversed without explicit Staff approval.

Medical

6.6 | MedRP Standards

Medical Roleplay (MedRP) may be administered by a Character upon other Characters. The level of care should be parallel to a Character's accepted skill level, regardless of mechanical stats. Medical RP should strive to be spiritually consistent to real life procedures and treatment. Medical RP is not a substitute for RevivalRP.

Medical

6.7 | Injuries

In any event of mechanical or story-based injury or death to a Character (PVP, PVE, narrative), the Player of the Character may select injuries of any relevant kind as well as non-zombifying infections. Chosen injuries must be promptly communicated to the treating personnel via in-character means or OOC communications. Injuries may not be chosen by personnel treating the Character, as this would constitute a violation of the 2.1 | Powergaming provision. Chosen injuries must be appropriately depicted in behavior by the afflicted Character and the Character must live with the injuries for a reasonable amount of in-game time.

Medical

6.8 | SurgeryRP Standards

Surgery Roleplay necessitates the following:

  • A sterile space
  • Appropriate medical equipment and appliances
  • At least one (1) Character with an explicit background in human surgery.
If a Character attempts to perform SurgeryRP without the above, or if the surgery is considered unrealistic under Staff discretion, the patient will be forced to accept an injury or Character Death, dependent on severity. All SurgeryRP requires the opening of a ticket prior to the act.

Revival

6.9 | RevRP Provisions

A Staff-approved Character may perform RevivalRP. All other Characters are prohibited from performing RevivalRP. RevivalRP is subject to stricter checks on MedicalRP quality. When RevivalRP is performed on a Player Character, the NLR provisions are nullified and the Character may return to the scene of death.

In the event that a Player Character is mechanically killed via PvP, a RevRP ticket may nullify the loss of a PVP death. This ticket must be opened within a narratively brief time period after the mechanical death.

All MedRP Standards and Injuries provisions still apply for all involved parties.

7.0 - Character Guidelines
Creation

7.1 | Restricted Roles

Players that wish to create certain types of Characters may be subject to Staff review prior to play.
Restricted backgrounds include:

  • RevRP-qualified Medical Personnel
  • Active-duty Military
  • Biological Research
  • Criminals
  • Physical Disability and/or Terminal Illness


Players must make a ticket for any role that may fall under restriction.

Creation

7.2 | Provision on Portrayals of Mental Illness

Players are encouraged to stray away from portraying mental illness through a character without appropriate levels of research and understanding for each condition. Characters that caricaturize, stereotype, or otherwise portray any mental illness in a distasteful light will be subject to a forced re-write at Staff discretion. Under extreme circumstances, Players may receive corrective action.

Creation

7.3 | Character Biography

All Characters are subject to a review by Staff prior to play. Each biography must be proofread for grammatical mistakes and consistency with established Lore. All Characters MUST be 18 years or older. All writing must be original. Collaboration is permitted.

Under no circumstance is a Player allowed to use generative AI or an LLM in the creation of their Character.

Creation

7.4 | Character Faceclaims

All Characters must have a faceclaim attached to their biography post. Drawn faceclaims are permissable, provided that the art is made by the author and original.

Under no circumstance is a Player allowed to use generative AI or an LLM in the creation of their Character.

Creation

7.5 | Re-Use of Characters

Re-use of Characters within the server is disallowed. Players are encouraged to write new and original Characters for each season. Characters may not be copies from existing media; all work must be inspired and original.

Staff

7.6 | Staff Discretion

Staff hold final decision on any Character deemed inappropriate, distasteful, or unfitting for the season's setting. It is recommended for the Player to consult others regarding their intentions for a Character.

Roleplay

7.7 | Character Continuity

Characters are expected to maintain a reasonable degree of consistency. This includes injuries, bites, and death state.

8.0 - Buildings and Property
Structures

8.1 | Player-Built Structure Regulations

All Player-built structures must follow sensible structural integrity principles. Size and area of the structure or compound must be in reasonable proportion to the number of Players residing within. Builds must be in line with 'realism' and must not pose unreasonable difficulty to breach.

Structures

8.2 | Protected Structures

Player-built structures are only considered protected under certain conditions.

  • All doorways must be secured with locks or two-tile-tall obstructions.
  • All windows must be barricaded or have intact glass panes.
  • There must be at least one sign or note left upon the exterior indicating ownership.

In a protected structure, non-owners are disallowed from tampering, stealing, or raiding without proper initiation.

Structures

8.3 | Protected Compounds

Player-built compounds are only considered protected under a certain set of conditions.

  • All entrances must be secured with locks or two-tile-tall obstructions.
  • Compound walls must encapsulate the space.
  • There must be at least one sign or note left upon the exterior indicating ownership.

In a protected compound, non-owners are disallowed from tampering, stealing, or raiding without proper initiation.

Structures

8.4 | Road Constructions

Players are permitted to construct bases on bridges and roads only if there exists an alternative route for non-allied players to traverse via paved road. Micro-structures, including roadblocks, are not permitted without explicit Staff approval.

Structures

8.5 | Abandoned Structures

Players may claim another Player's structure as their own 14 real-life days after informing Staff. Should Staff observe no activity during the waiting period, or if the previous owner confirms a renouncement of the territory, the structure or compound is considered abandoned and the transfer of ownership will be confirmed.

Players abandoning bases are encouraged to pre-emptively report the act to Staff via ticket.

Structures

8.6 | Multi-Basing

Players may not claim multiple structures, landmarks, or compounds without explicit Staff approval. Players who wish to create outposts or secondary claims must inform Staff via ticket. If a claim is adjacent to the Player's existing protected structure, it is considered part of the structure and does not require Staff approval if the entire build abides the regulations outlined in provision 8.1.

Vehicles

8.7 | Vehicle Claims

Player-owned vehicles are only considered protected under a certain set of conditions.

  • The glovebox contains a note with the owner's name.
  • The vehicle is not obstructing public roadways.
  • The owner is an active Player.
If the vehicle is located in a protected compound, it is exempt from the above conditions and considered protected.

Vehicles

8.8 | Vehicle Disassembly

Players may not disassemble vehicles outside of a protected structure. All vehicles must be re-located to a protected structure or compound prior to disassembly of any parts.

Vehicles with disassembled or missing parts may not be returned to a non-claimed space.

9.0 - Factions & Groups
Groups

9.1 | Group Restrictions

Players are permitted to have a Group of up to six people total, including the owner, without Staff pre-approval. Groups may not bypass this limit without re-establishing as a faction. See 9.3.

Groups formed as an attempt to bypass membership limits are strictly prohibited.

Groups

9.2 | Group Alliances

Players are permitted to form alliances against a Faction or NPC-Group.

Players are not permitted to form alliances against Player-led Groups without explicit Staff approval.

Faction

9.3 | Faction Restrictions

Player-led Groups are permitted to establish as a faction under a certain set of conditions.

  • The Faction has an appropriate narrative purpose or objective.
  • The Faction has seven or more members.
  • The Faction has a cumulative total of no more than three previous-violations from members within its roster.
  • All Faction members agree to follow directives from Staff regarding Faction-wide actions.

Roleplay

9.4 | Exceptions to Faction & Group Provisions

Rare exceptions may be made to the provisions within this category if season lore or existing Player Character dynamics permit.

In the instance of a QZ-themed season, all members residing within the QZ territory shall be considered loosely-associated and may collaborate against NPC-Groups or NPC-Factions.
