Player's Handbook

Welcome to SledgehammerRP! Below is every rule the server currently employs to ensure a fair, compelling, and safe experience for all. Rules are subject to change and interpretation by staff.

1.0 - Discord & Community Guidelines

Community

1.1 | General Conduct

Harassment, toxicity, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, racism, ableism, and all other forms of bigotry are not tolerated in this space. Debates upon political, social, or otherwise controversial issues from the real-world are prohibited.

Community

1.2 | OOC/IC Civility

All Discord channels are considered OOC unless explicitly marked otherwise. Do not bring IC or in-game conflicts into Discord. Collaborate and cooperate with fellow players/writers through in-game channels.

Staff

1.3 | Staff Authority

ALL staff decisions are final. If one questions or disagrees with a staff member's decision, appeal privately via the ticket system.

Discord

1.4 | English Only

SledgehammerRP is an English-only server. In order to enforce policies and moderation, all players and members of the Discord are expected to speak English.

Discord

1.5 | Prior Conduct In Other Spaces

If a Player has an evidence-based history of problematic conduct in other spaces or servers, staff reserve the right to revoke the Player's whitelist and perform a removal of the Player at any time.

Discord

1.6 | Follow Discord's Terms of Service

Do not share content that violates Discord's terms of service. This includes, but is not limited to, piracy, distribution of NSFW content, doxxing, or other illegal activities.

2.0 - Roleplay Standards

Roleplay

Initiate Before You Engage

A clear RP initiation is required before any hostile action against another survivor — a demand, a threat, a warning shot. No surprise executions without a roleplayed reason your character could act on.

3.0 Roleplay Standards

Roleplay Standard

Stay In Character

Once you're loaded in, you're your survivor. Break character only through the designated OOC channel or the /ooc command — never in local chat where other survivors can "hear" it.

Roleplay Standard

Perma-Death Is Permanent

If your survivor is confirmed dead through roleplay, that character is retired. Roll a new survivor and re-enter Knox County through the standard onboarding process.

Looting & Economy

Looting & Economy

No Meta-Looting

Don't use map knowledge or out-of-character information to beeline valuable loot. If your survivor hasn't found a location in-character, they don't know what's inside.

Looting & Economy

Base Claims Are First-Come

A base is claimed once it's barricaded and marked with a claim sign. Raiding an active, claimed base requires an RP reason and follows the server's raid rules — no exceptions.
